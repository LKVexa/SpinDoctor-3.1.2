Theoretical Foundations of Quantum Computation: Equations of States, Gates, and Circuit Architectures
Mathematical Representation of Quantum States and Geometries
The rigorous mathematical formulation of quantum states dictates the evolution, measurement, and error mitigation of quantum information. At the most fundamental level, the state of a two-level quantum system, or qubit, is represented as a unit vector within a separable complex Hilbert space. However, theoretical quantum computation requires a robust framework to account for mixed states, multi-qubit entanglement, and high-dimensional generalizations across diverse symmetry groups.
