SpinDoctor 3.1.2

New here? Read HANDGUIDE.pdf (or HANDGUIDE.md) first: a plain-language guide
with quick start, command reference, folder runs, limits and troubleshooting.
What changed: CHANGELOG.md and Release-Notes.txt. Audit: AUDIT_3.1.2.md.

Launch SpinDoctor\BOOT.cmd from this folder. BOOT verifies and materializes
the selected versioned TIFF/GIF carrier before opening the terminal.

Measurement CSV: upload, inspect columns, then explicitly choose a numeric
target and predictor columns in Fit measured data. The returned equation,
coefficients, fit statistics and residuals are empirical and use the supplied
measurements. They do not establish a physical law or unseen prediction accuracy.

Gate-list CSV: upload, inspect, then choose Run attached circuit. The declared
gates are simulated through the native engine with the file's shots/basis hints.
CSV gate programs default to 1,024 shots in Z unless specified otherwise.

CSV companions are available for command, sequence, file and PDF report exports.
Report CSVs contain typed nested native values; input CSV tables have their own
schemas. Exporting reports does not repeat calculation or verification.
Equation reports contain editable plain text and conventional mathematical
notation, with coefficient mappings or declared circuit operators/local matrices.
The local equation editor supports copying/resetting a draft; edits do not change
the native result or its receipt. Explicitly Fit/Import/Run changed source data
to obtain a new native result.

See CSV-Input-Guide.txt and Examples for templates. Reports contains a combined
PDF with native equation graphs, circuit diagrams, histograms and Bloch spheres,
plus matching TXT/CSV reports. Measurement examples are synthetic demonstration
data. Reports\Inputs contains the exact bytes used for the saved report;
the rotated example explicitly declares eight shots, while Bell uses 1,024.

The 3.1.1 candidate and its history remain preserved (this is a separate release).

(identical outcomes to 3.1.1), the folder chug series (103/103 expected rows)
and every hand-guide example. The 128-file x 120-command stress folder and
maximum verification ceilings were not run.
Reports (3.1.1 examples) are unchanged.
